## 5. WARRANTY, EXPECTATIONS & AFTERCARE ADVICE

### Chemical Warranty & Flush-Out Guidelines
* **Standard Warranty:** All Australian Chemicals have a 3 Month Warranty when Sprayed.
* **Effective Duration:** The value if sprayed correctly will be 6–12 months depending on any conducive conditions.
* **Flush Out Period:** Please allow 6 weeks for your treatment to work.
  * Pests will be flushed out and will continue to be flushed out for up to 6 weeks.
  * Living across or next to bushland will result in more insects regularly as they get blown in by the wind.
  * Insects will still be affected by the applied chemicals and die when they make contact with the insecticide.
  * Pests must come into contact with the insecticide for them to die (insecticides do not work by smell).

### Estimated Time to Die After Contact
* **1 Month Post-Treatment:** Up to 2 Days estimate to die
* **2 Months Post-Treatment:** Up to 4–6 Days estimate to die
* **3 Months Post-Treatment:** Up to 6–8 Days estimate to die
